Creative Statement

As DJ and electronic musician, my approach to music is based informed by the history of house and techno music, and formed in digital synthesis and sample based manipulations, searching for the strange and groovy frontiers of amplified, distorted, temporal, archival and found sounds. With writing I aim to locate and examine how technology and cultural works enable each other as they evolve. I am interested in the history of modern cultural movements, the people and events that shape and define them, be it with music, food, or other mediums of expression. I also like to learn and examine the social and economic realities of the technologies and methods used and developed, how they are designed, and how they are appropriated beyond their initial intentions by later adopters.
